# Account onboarding

Mode: Operate. Extend Palla's established light-only visual system. Audience: newly authenticated players and organizers. Task: choose where to start, then provide a name. Immediate organizer activation is confirmed. Layout A in `mocks/onboarding-options.png` is approved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: two short steps, with both mode choices visible and account identity shared across modes.

OWN-WORLD: pale lavender canvas, white unselected choices, pale lime selection with an olive outline, lime primary action, Hanken Grotesk headings and existing controls. No new imagery or palette.

STORY: select Player or Organizer, continue, enter a first name and optional last name, save and reach the matching home. Organizer access is immediate. Errors stay inline and drafts survive back navigation within the flow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: centered Palla header and step count; left-aligned heading and description; two stacked radio panels with line icons and trailing radio circles; helper and Continue action below. On mobile use the approved portrait proportions with scroll rather than clipping at large type; desktop centers the same form.

FORM: approved stacked choices A; explicit user decision overrides random concept generation. Back appears on the name step; the first step offers sign out instead of navigating into account creation while authenticated.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
